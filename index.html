Company Presentation

Umbrella Corporation is a multinational powerhouse, founded in 1968 by Dr. Oswell E. Spencer, Edward Ashford, and James Marcus. With operations spanning pharmaceuticals, medical equipment, biotechnology, and advanced genetic research, Umbrella remains at the forefront of innovation.

Our mission is to protect and enhance life through science — a vision made possible by our state-of-the-art laboratories and dedicated research teams. Umbrella's symbol represents global protection, sheltering humanity beneath the canopy of progress.

Umbrella — Our business is life itself.
